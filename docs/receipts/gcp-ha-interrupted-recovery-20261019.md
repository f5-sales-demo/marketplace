# Private GCP HA interrupted-success recovery and cleanup

The private three-node Montréal campaign submitted one exact eighteen-create
saved binary, then stopped the parent after its Terraform child succeeded.
Recovery refused the still-live recorded executor without changing ten protected
artifact bindings. After the executor stopped, recovery verified all eighteen
original identities and reconciled the consumed attempt without replay.
Repeated resume confirmed completion. The original binary and receipt were
archived before a follow-up plan reported eighteen no-op resource changes.

Two consecutive observations matched the original identities of three ONLINE
nodes and both physical NIC joins on every node. Component status, independent
service inventory and global health were unavailable in those observations.
Their absence remains unknown health; zero returned rows cannot establish
service coverage. This campaign does not qualify exhaustive HA health.

A separate exact eighteen-delete review bound cleanup to the original attempt,
verified live identities and stable owning state. One destroy submission ended
with an empty final destroy plan and stable state. Independent checks found all
fourteen cloud objects and four XC objects absent. Repeated cleanup resume
confirmed all eighteen absences without another deletion. The inventory included
three VMs, three explicitly managed disks, two networks, two subnets and four
interface firewall rules.

The campaign froze fresh bundles after passing exact-head CI. Platform and
Terraform executed from those installed bundles; the GCloud source adapter
prepared inputs and inspected ownership. The retained GCloud bundle records
provenance but was not loaded as the public GCloud runtime. Public lifecycle admission
remains single-node pending owning immutable API qualification and fresh public
installed/live acceptance. Raw identities, observations, bootstrap material,
credentials, plans and state remain restricted outside Git.
