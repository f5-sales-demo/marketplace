reviewed inventory, apply callbacks and insertion references. CE and observer

failing-first regression reproduced six unnecessary cloud reads in three identity
checks. Same-byte client-file replacement permanently retires the lease. A delayed
cloud regression failed against preserved earlier source and passes with the
repair under the unchanged fifteen-second startup limit.
