## Installed starting point

The published Mac binary at `/opt/homebrew/Caskroom/xcsh/23.8.0/bin/xcsh`
loaded installed Salesforce 2.3.12 for a fixture-only native capture.
All six tools were registered. Independent Herdr observations matched every
requested row in 450 expanded viewport pages across 40/76/120/153 columns,
both themes and Unicode/ASCII/Nerd presets. The 153-column describe result
contained 420 lines; the final 41-row page was clamped to offset 379.

The capture harness retired its owned execution for cancellation, simulated
context replacement and shutdown. Each scenario aborted execution, rejected
late updates and observed zero repaints during the 240 ms retirement window.
Rejected updates numbered 2, 1 and 1 respectively. These are harness-controlled
retirements, not proof of interactive agent context replacement. The capture
completed and its task-owned pane and terminal control lease were retired.
Raw fixture observations remain private. No Salesforce service probe was used
for these results.

places and labels their unverified currency. Org-status guidance uses the safe
native tool, and authentication recovery directs the human to canonical setup.

completed executions reject late updates without changing a replacement.

Failing-first regressions reproduced the lifecycle dollar abbreviation,
unsafe org-command examples, incomplete session-expiry guidance and missing
completion retirement. Focused Salesforce tests pass: 328 tests and 2,173
assertions. Shared-runtime qualification passes: 24 tests and 21,848 assertions.
Marketplace contracts pass: 78 tests and 668 assertions before the additional
completion regression; the final native/ownership run passes 7 tests and

installed dependency graph, privacy scan, secret scan and changed guidance
Markdown lint pass. Inventory reconciliation passes with 23 plugins, 324 active
surfaces and 2,757 tasks, preserving prior receipts and invalidating changed
source fingerprints.
